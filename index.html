Overview

Scenestar Scenes Ltd covers construction, smart home, security, IT solutions and renewable energy. This proposal is for developers, property owners and home owners.

It outlines the design, supply, installation, programming, testing and commissioning of smart home and property automation. The system gives you convenient control of all lighting, air conditioning, security, surveillance, entertainment and other existing devices and appliances through a mobile app, a voice assistant and remote access.

Areas covered in the package

  • Main entrance
  • Living room
  • Dining room
  • Kitchen
  • Bedroom

Goals & objectives

The proposed automation solutions aim to:

  1. Automate lighting and HVAC systems in the property.
  2. Provide remote control from anywhere in the world.
  3. Monitor energy consumption and improve energy efficiency.
  4. Enhance security and safety through smart monitoring of flood, fire and theft.
  5. Integrate all smart devices for use in a single application.
  6. Provide a scalable platform for future expansion and improvement, in line with future technologies.

Smart features

What each feature does once it is installed.

Comfort & energy
Smart lighting
Lights that set any mood in a zone, based on the user's aesthetic preferences and requirements.
Light control
Energy-saving smart control of all indoor and outdoor lights.
HVAC control
Smart control for installed air conditioners and extractors.
Curtain automation
Curtains controlled by voice, phone, remote control and cloud connectivity.
Gadget control
Intelligent power management, automated convenience routines, and safety and maintenance overrides, with voice and remote control of all appliances and gadgets in a space.
Machine control
Smart, centralized control of all equipment for seamless operation, monitoring and scheduling, including automated oversight and power management for every machine, gadget and piece of equipment in a zone.
Sensors
Presence sensors switch lights on with motion. Contact sensors secure doors and trigger guest-welcome and routine actions. Humidity sensors trigger the air conditioner based on the required room temperature.
Security & safety
Surveillance
4K cameras with real-time HD footage and 365 days of storage backup.
Security system
Unifies perimeter defense, video surveillance, access control and hazard detection in one centralized, real-time management network.
Laser fencing
A perimeter defense system that flags breaches and triggers connected security responses without obstructing the property's appearance.
Fire aware
Smoke, thermal and optical sensors with automated building responses to detect fire hazards early, contain their spread and protect lives and property.
Flood aware
Early detection of floods and water leaks, with automated triggers that protect the property from damage caused by heavy rainfall, rising outdoor water levels or severe internal leaks.
Access & entry
Access control
Doors unlock by facial recognition, fingerprint, passcode, palm, remote or access card.
Gate automation
Pedestrian and vehicle access control at the property perimeter. Opening, closing and verification are automated to streamline traffic flow, strengthen perimeter security and remove manual gate operation.
Sound, voice & communication
Sound automation
Sound from the entertainment unit is processed through quality amplifiers and in-ceiling speakers positioned strategically in each zone.
Streaming assistant
Music commands by voice and AirPlay.
Voice assistant
Hands-free control of amenities and facility services through natural spoken commands.
Intercom
Real-time two-way audio communication between zones in a structure.
Wi-Fi optimization
Stable internet across the whole structure.
Power & backup
Smart home backup
Renewable electricity keeps smart-home communication with the user running by supplying power to the required smart devices, even when all supply (grid, inverter and generator) fails.
Functional backup
A smart load system that prioritizes critical loads during an outage, switching seamlessly between solar, battery and grid power so essential devices keep running efficiently and consumption stays managed.
Total backup
A system that carries the entire energy demand during an outage, switching seamlessly between solar, battery and grid power to keep all appliances, gadgets and devices running.
Automatic transfer switch (ATS)
Switches between grid, generator and inverter automatically, without human input or errors.
Automatic voltage regulator (AVR)
Stabilizes and regulates the voltage supplied to the property and protects all appliances, machines and gadgets from electrical surges.
